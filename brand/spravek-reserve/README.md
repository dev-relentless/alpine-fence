# Spravek Reserve — Brand Assets

Spravek Reserve, a DBA of Relentless Equipment LLC. Luxury vehicle rentals · Utah.

Drop assets into the folders below. Keep source files (SVG, AI, PSD, RAW) alongside exports so they can be re-edited later.

| Folder | What goes here |
| --- | --- |
| `logos/` | The beveled V mark, one-color V, stacked and horizontal lockups, badge, favicon. Prefer SVG; add PNG exports at 512 / 1024 / 2048 px. |
| `vehicles/` | Photos and video of the fleet: SL 63 S E Performance, S 580, F-350 Platinum Plus Dually. Use one subfolder per vehicle. |
| `marketing/` | Campaign posters, social templates, ad copy, the "Allocation" key cards, email headers. |
| `documents/` | Rental agreement, chauffeur service terms, brand sheet, DBA filing, insurance certificates (no secrets or account numbers). |
| `code/` | Standalone HTML/SVG from the design canvas (`logo.html`, `poster.html`, `spravek-v-mark*.svg`) and any future landing-page source. |

## Brand quick reference

- **Accent (gold):** `#C8A96A` champagne
- **Accent (silver):** beveled silver, matches the Relentless/Spravek V mark
- **Tagline:** Luxury Vehicle Rentals · Utah
- **Vehicle roles:** The Roadster (SL 63 S E Performance) · The Arrival (S 580, self-drive or chauffeured) · The Statement (F-350 Platinum Plus Dually)
- **Legal line:** "Spravek Reserve, a DBA of Relentless Equipment LLC"

## Naming

Use lowercase kebab-case: `spravek-v-mark.svg`, `s580-exterior-front-01.jpg`, `allocation-poster-4x5.png`.
