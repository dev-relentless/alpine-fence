# Alpine Fence & Deck — Photo Shot List (Handoff)

**Audience:** photographer / content team
**Goal:** capture & deliver every image the live site references, named exactly as listed so they drop straight into `/public/images/...` and go live.

**Rules:**
- Filenames are **case-sensitive**. Use exactly what's listed.
- Default format: **JPG**, sRGB, quality 80–85, optimized < 350 KB where possible.
- Use lowercase, dashes only — no spaces, no underscores (except where listed).
- Before/After pairs must be the **same yard, same camera angle, same focal length**.
- When in doubt, shoot landscape orientation and crop to the listed aspect ratio.
