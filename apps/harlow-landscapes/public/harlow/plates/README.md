# Harlow — Homepage Photo Shot List

Placeholders on the homepage reference the files below. Replace each with a
final photograph at (or larger than) the target dimensions. All shots should
share a consistent tonal register: warm neutral, long shadows, no busy skies,
no people. Think Reed Hilderbrand, Hoerr Schaudt, Janice Parker portfolios.

## Register
- **Time of day:** late golden hour, blue hour, or after dusk with warm interior light.
- **Weather:** overcast diffuse OR bright late-afternoon. Avoid harsh midday.
- **Colour:** warm neutrals — stone, gravel, dark evergreen, taupe grasses. Reserve saturation for one warm accent (interior light, brass fixture).
- **Framing:** wide, symmetrical or one-third; low horizon; architecture partly visible where possible.
- **Never:** people, cars, hoses, signage, brand marks, obvious property addresses.

- **Subject:** estate terrace or approach at dusk. Warm interior light spilling onto stone. Mature planting in the foreground, architecture in the middle ground.
